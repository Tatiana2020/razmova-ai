# Razmova

Free Private AI Language Practice & On-Device Chat

[razmova.ca](https://razmova.ca)

Become a more confident communicator with Razmova, your private local AI companion for language learning, strengthening your communication skills, and meaningful conversations. Practice speaking English, Spanish, or French with an AI partner that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. The AI downloads once (about 1.8 GB), then works offline forever: free, no account, no subscription.

## Choose Your Partner

- **Razmova (English), Sofía (Spanish), Romy (French):** beginner (A1/A2). Short sentences, everyday words, a patient pace.
- **Emma (English), Javier (Spanish), Gabriel (French):** intermediate (B1/B2). Longer, natural conversations about travel, food, films, and culture.
- **Juan Carlos:** a friendly coach for confident communication. Get ready for a presentation, make small talk easier, or talk a tricky conversation through. Sessions are in English.

## Speak and Listen

Tap the microphone and speak with any partner. Your words appear as text you can edit before sending. Tap the speaker to hear any reply read aloud, word by word, at normal speed or slowed down. Every partner speaks in a voice that suits them, using free iOS voices that also work offline. Stuck on a word? You can translate any message, fully offline once you've downloaded the languages in Settings > Apps > Translate.

## Built for Real-World Confidence

**Speak Without Anxiety:** Practice everyday conversations, vocabulary, and grammar with empathetic AI personas that feel like real conversation partners. Perfect for language learners looking to bridge the gap between studying and speaking.

**100% Judgment-Free Sandbox:** Vent about a tough day at work, complain about your boss, or prepare for an upcoming presentation. Razmova provides a completely safe, private space to build communication skills on your own terms.

**Zero Cloud Footprint:** Because your conversations are processed locally using state-of-the-art on-device LLM architecture, your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, absolute privacy. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://razmova.ca/privacy-policy).

## What You Need

Razmova needs an iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.6 or later and at least 6 GB of memory (RAM). It checks at first launch and tells you right away, before any download starts. The AI model itself downloads over Wi-Fi. Voice input requires iOS 26 or later.

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, you can switch to it in Razmova's settings and remove the downloaded model to free up about 1.8 GB. Either way, everything stays on your device.
